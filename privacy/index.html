3.1 Cloud-Spielstand (immer)

Clawit! speichert deinen Spielstand auf einem Server. So bleibt deine Sammlung auf einem zweiten Gerät erhalten, und der Spieltag richtet sich nach der Serverzeit statt nach der Geräteuhr. Dabei werden verarbeitet:

  • eine zufällige Installations-ID, damit Versuche pro Tag über mehrere Geräte richtig gezählt werden,
  • technisch bedingt deine IP-Adresse und der Zeitpunkt jeder Anfrage (Server-Protokolle).

Nach einer Neuinstallation findet die App deinen Spielstand unter iOS über den Schlüsselbund wieder. Unter Android klappt das nur, wenn die Android-Sicherung deines Google-Kontos eingeschaltet ist.

Die App entfernt vor dem Senden die Spieler-ID, den Gerätenamen und jede Protokollzeile, die eine Kennung enthält (Spieler-, Installations- oder Werbe-ID). 3. What data is processed

3.1 Cloud save (always)

Clawit! stores your game progress on a server. This keeps your collection on a second device, and the game day follows server time instead of the device clock. The following is processed:

The player ID, sign-in token and install ID are stored on your device because this is strictly necessary for the service (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

3.2 Age question

After your first win Clawit! asks for your year of birth. It is stored on your device only, is not part of the cloud save and is never sent anywhere. The app only derives whether you are under 13, under 16 or older.

Because only the year is known, not the day, Clawit! always assumes your birthday this year has not happened yet. Someone turning 16 this year therefore counts as 15 until the year ends. The same applies to the age of 13.

The limit of 16 applies the same in every country. In Germany it is the legal minimum age for giving consent (Art. 8 GDPR). Some countries set lower limits; Clawit! applies the stricter one everywhere.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is protecting minors with as little data as possible and obtaining consent only from people allowed to give it themselves. The year of birth is stored on your device only, because the app cannot apply age-appropriate settings without it (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
Retention: on your device until you uninstall the app. “Delete Save” deliberately keeps the year of birth so the age question cannot simply be answered again.

3.3 Play statistics (16+ and only with your consent)

If you are 16 or older, Clawit! asks right after the year of birth whether it may send pseudonymous play statistics. Only on “Yes” is GameAnalytics started. Before that it is not initialised and makes no connection. Until you answer, the app keeps the events of this first session in memory only. On “Yes” they are sent, on “No” they are discarded.

The following is then transmitted:

  • gameplay events, e.g. day started, try started, prize won, collection opened, first-session progress,
  • session length,
  • technical device data: model, OS, app version, language,

3.5 Crash reports

When Clawit! crashes or hits an error, a report is sent so I can find and fix it. It contains:

  • the error message and the place in the code,
  • the last steps in the app before the error (internal log lines),
  • device model, OS and app version.

Before sending, the app removes the player ID, the device name and every log line containing an identifier (player, install or advertising ID). The Sentry component creates its own random install ID on your device (not the one from section 3.1); the app does not send it. Only in a crash deep in the system (not in the game code) can the component attach it to the report itself. Sentry then removes it before storing, so I cannot see how many devices an error affects. No screenshots or screen recordings are sent. Your IP address is necessarily transmitted when sending; Sentry derives the country from it and does not store the IP address itself.

Provider: Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry), USA, as processor; data stored in the EU (Frankfurt) (Sentry’s privacy policy). Access from the US cannot be ruled out (see section 5).
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is a stable and secure app. You may object (section 6). A crash report is kept on your device and sent on the next start, because the app is terminated at the moment of the crash; without this storage every report would be lost (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). There are no usage or session statistics through Sentry.
Retention: at most 30 days.

6. Your rights and erasure

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You may object to processing based on my legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21). You may withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). Withdrawal applies to the future; processing before it remains lawful.

Deleting it yourself: “Settings ▸ Delete Save” deletes, on the server, your game account (player ID and sign-in data) and your stored save, and on your device the save. Your year of birth, settings and privacy answers stay on the device until you uninstall the app; the year of birth deliberately, so the age question cannot simply be answered again. If the device is offline, the app completes the deletion the next time it is online and syncs nothing until then. Not deleted immediately are:

  • server logs and database backups at Supabase, which expire on their own after at most 7 days,
  • data held by GameAnalytics, Sentry, Google and bunny.net, which expires after the periods in section 4.

Requests by email: write to clawit.app@gmail.com. So that I can find your data, please include your player ID. It is on the last line of Settings; tapping it copies it. I reply within one month.

Data held by GameAnalytics, Sentry, Google and bunny.net carries no player ID, and I cannot link it to a person. I can therefore only have it erased or report on it if you give me additional details that allow it to be identified (Art. 11 GDPR). You can also contact Google directly about ad data.

Complaints: you may lodge a complaint with any data protection supervisory authority. The one responsible for me is the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Ludwig-Erhard-Straße 22, 20459 Hamburg, Germany (datenschutz-hamburg.de).

Obligation to provide data: you do not have to provide any data. Without a year of birth the app starts no ads. Without an internet connection you keep playing; the cloud save catches up once you are back online.
